01 / VISION

What I Actually Believe In.

I want URJA to be the society where you actually grew.

Not just "attended events" but built skills, found your people, and left genuinely better than you came. A place where freshers aren't lost, seniors aren't burnt out, and every department knows exactly why they matter.

I'm not here to reinvent URJA. I'm here to make what already works work better, work consistently, and work for everyone.

That's the URJA I want to build.
01

Show Up Every Week

Not just during events. Consistency is the foundation weekly check ins, regular content, steady momentum. URJA should never go silent.

02

Give People Real Ownership

Not tasks responsibility. When a fresher leads a campaign and it works, they don't just help the society. They discover what they're capable of.

03

Measure Everything, Assume Nothing

If we ran an event, how did it actually do? If we posted content, did anyone care? Numbers aren't cold they're honest. And honest is how we improve.

04 / DEPARTMENTS

Department
Playbooks.

Not job descriptions action plans. What actually changes under my leadership.

01

Marketing Department

A marketing vertical that plans ahead, measures everything, and knows its audience inside out.

Pre Event Countdown System

  • 10 days before every event: structured hype calendar with daily content mapped
  • Day 10: teaser. Day 7: speaker reveal. Day 3: registration push. Day 1: final call
  • Same proven formula every time no reinventing the wheel for each event

Monthly Marketing Review

  • One focused session per month: what posted, what performed, what flopped
  • Review top posts vs bottom posts extract patterns, not just vibes
  • Next month's content direction decided with data on the table

Audience Deep Dive

  • Once per semester, full analysis: who follows URJA? What age? What college?
  • Which posts get saved vs liked? What content drives profile visits?
  • Build a real audience profile stop guessing what "might work"

Public Relations Department

PR that doesn't just count collabs it rates them, learns from them, and gets better every time.

Collab Quality Rating

  • After every society collab: rate 1 5 on reach gained, effort required, responsiveness
  • Over time, build a clear picture of which partnerships are actually worth repeating
  • Ranked "priority partners" list handed over to next year's team

Outreach Response Tracker

  • Log every outreach attempt: who was contacted, when, response time, outcome
  • Over time, learn the best channels, best timing, and which societies ghost
  • Data replaces guesswork stop repeating failed approaches

PR Milestone Recognition

  • Clear targets: 10 collabs closed = shoutout, 20 = story feature, 30 = certificate
  • Tangible goals motivate the team beyond "just do more outreach"
  • Creates healthy competition and visible progress tracking

Corporate Communication

Sponsorship that runs year round with a clear system, real follow through, and no dropped leads.

Sponsor Feedback Survey

  • After every event, send sponsors a 3 question survey: Was it worth it? What would you change? Would you return?
  • Their answers shape next year's pitch sponsors feel heard, deals improve
  • Transforms guesswork into a data informed sponsorship strategy

Cold Outreach Playbook

  • Documented email templates, LinkedIn messages, and phone scripts ready to use
  • New members start outreach on Day 1 without being trained from scratch
  • Consistent tone and professionalism across every touchpoint

Weekly Pipeline Review

  • Every Monday, 10 minute check: how many sponsors in each stage? Who needs follow up?
  • No lead goes cold because "someone forgot to reply"
  • Keeps momentum constant especially critical in the Confluentia buildup months

Earned Trust,

Trust isn't given with a position. It's earned by being there when it matters. I'm trusted by people across every department, not just my own circle.

I've mentored members who were fading out, brought inactive ones back into the fold, and made sure people had real ownership, not just tasks. I've always been the first to show up and the last to leave. Not for the credit. For the people.
